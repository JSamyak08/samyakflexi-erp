PHASE 1 Plant Configuration, Legal Profile & User Roles (Day 1)
  • Company Profile & Letterhead Series (`Letterhead & Signatures`)
    Verify legal name (*Samyak International Ltd*), GSTIN, CIN, Plant Unit address, upload high-res header/footer artwork, and configure doc prefixes (`QUO-`, `ORD-`, `PO-`, `GRN-`, `DC-`).
  • Digital Signature Authorizations (`Letterhead & Signatures`)
    Upload high-contrast transparent signatures for Plant Head, Sales Manager, QC Chemist, and Authorized Dispatch Officer.
  • Printing Press Master Directory (`Printing Presses`)
    Register all Rotogravure Presses (e.g. *Press 1 - 8 Color*, *Press 2 - 9 Color*), mechanical speeds (m/min), max web width (mm), color decks, and assigned shift bays.
  • Departmental User Provisioning & RBAC (`User Management`)
    Provision user credentials for all roles (Admin, Plant Manager, Production Manager, Printing Operators, Store Manager, QC Chemist, Sales Manager, Purchase Manager).
PHASE 2 Commercial Master Directories (Day 1 - Day 2)
  • Vendor Master Ingestion (`Vendors`)
    Register raw material film manufacturers (e.g. *Chiripal, Polyplex, Cosmo*), Ink makers (*Hubergroup, DIC, Toyo*), Adhesive suppliers (*Henkel, Bostik*), and Cylinder engravers. Input GSTIN, contact emails, credit days, and plant locations.
  • Client Master Directory (`Clients`)
    Input client company names, brand divisions, GSTIN, registered billing address, default consignee delivery destinations, credit terms (e.g. 30 Days Net), and transport preferences.
  • Standard Terms & Conditions Presets (`Document Settings`)
    Pre-populate legal payment terms, freight clauses, tolerance margins (±10%), and cylinder maintenance clauses for Quotation, PO, and Delivery Challan PDFs.

Sequential Master Data Ingestion Pipeline (Phase 3 to Phase 6) Technical Pre-Press, Inventory Barcoding & Live Shop Floor Execution
PHASE 3 Pre-Press Technical Specs & Tooling Master (Day 2 - Day 3)
  • Ink Master & Solid Content Library (`Ink Management`)
    Input product codes, ink shades (Process & Spot Pantone), ink type (Reverse/Surface/NTNK), solid content %, solid tolerance (±2%), and base rates (₹/kg).
  • Rotogravure Cylinder Master Database (`Cylinders`)
    Log SKU codes, total colors, cylinder repeat circumference (mm), print width (mm), face length, engraver name, physical rack location (e.g. *Rack C-04*), and cylinder proof PDFs.
  • Job Master Technical Directory (`Job Masters`)
    Input multi-layer substrate structure (e.g. *PET 12µ / METPET 12µ / Poly 40µ*), individual layer GSM, ink/adhesive GSM targets, cylinder repeat, pouch dimensions, and process routing.
PHASE 4 Warehouse Opening Stock & Barcode Labeling (Day 3 - Day 4)
  • Substrate Film Stock Items (`Inventory`)
    Configure master film categories: Plain PET, METPET, BOPP, Poly/PE, CPP, Alu Foil. Set widths (mm), micron (µ), density, and reorder levels.
  • Master Roll Physical Weighing & Barcode Tagging (`Store`)
    Connect digital scale, weigh all physical master film rolls in raw material warehouse, generate unique Roll Barcodes (`Alt+B`), and affix barcode tags to roll cores.
  • Ink Kitchen & Solvent Opening Inventory (`Ink Management`)
    Weigh all sealed and return ink drums, log opening stock (kg), and assign to designated Ink Room racks.
PHASE 5 Work Order Punching & Commercial Orders (Day 4 - Day 5)
  • Punch Active Manufacturing Work Orders (`Job Punching / Orders`)
    Input all pending and in-progress customer orders, link to approved Job Master SKUs, verify target order qty (kg), delivery dates, and auto-generated Bill of Materials (BOM).
  • Pending Purchase Orders (`Issued POs`)
    Enter ongoing raw material substrate, ink, or cylinder POs currently placed with vendors for live inward tracking.
PHASE 6 Printing Scheduler Live Floor Cutover (Day 5)
  • Load Ready Queue Sequence (`Printing Scheduler`)
    Sequence jobs by shade families (light to dark) and cylinder repeat width. Verify green readiness indicators for substrate and cylinder availability.
  • Lock Live Running Job & Begin Shop Floor Logging (`Printing Scheduler`)
    Operator clicks *Start Job* on mounted machine to activate real-time digital timer. Capture actual meters, ink GSM, and net kg output upon completion.
